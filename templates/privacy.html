Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Convertly ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). Convertly is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and when using our services. This Privacy Policy explains our information practices, what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Files You Upload: When you use Convertly to convert, translate, merge, or process files, we temporarily store your uploaded files on our servers to process them.
  • Voucher Codes: When you redeem a voucher code, we record the code for verification purposes.
  • Payment Information: PayPal handles all payment processing. We do NOT store credit card information directly. PayPal provides us with confirmation of successful payments.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • IP Address & User-Agent: To track usage quotas and prevent abuse, we create a fingerprint from your IP address and browser User-Agent.
  • Session Data: We store your conversion count, remaining quota, and daily translation limit server-side.
  • Log Files: Our servers log file names, conversion types, timestamps, and error messages for debugging and analytics.
  • Analytics: We may use anonymized analytics to understand how features are used.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your file conversions and transformations
  • To track and enforce usage quotas (free tier vs. paid)
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, and attacks
  • To improve our service and fix bugs
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To send you notifications about your account or service (emails, if applicable)

4. File Storage & Deletion

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Your files are not stored permanently.
  • Temporary Processing: Uploaded files are stored temporarily (typically 1-24 hours) only to process your conversion request.
  • Automatic Deletion: Files are automatically deleted after:
    • Successful download of your converted file, OR
    • 24 hours from upload (whichever comes first)
  • Immediate Deletion: You can request immediate deletion at any time.
  • No Backup: We do not create backups of your files. If you lose your converted file, we cannot restore it.

5. Data Security

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Passwords (for PDF protection) are NOT stored - they're processed and immediately discarded
  • Session tokens use cryptographic signing and HttpOnly cookies
  • Rate limiting and fingerprinting prevent unauthorized access
  • We maintain security logs but sanitize password data from logs

6. User Quotas & Tracking

To enforce fair usage (free tier: 3 conversions/day, premium tier: unlimited), we track:

  • Your IP address combined with your device's user-agent (browser fingerprint)
  • Number of conversions performed and remaining balance
  • Daily translation attempts
  • Voucher code usage

This data is server-side only and NOT relayed to third parties for marketing or profiling.

7. Cookies & Tracking

  • Session Cookies: Used to maintain your session state (login, converted files, etc.)
  • Pro Token Cookies: HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite cookies that verify your premium status
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or other third-party trackers
  • CAPTCHA: If enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile may set cookies (see Cloudflare's privacy policy)

8. Third-Party Services

  • PayPal: Handles payment processing. Their privacy policy: https://www.paypal.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: CAPTCHA service (if enabled). Their privacy statement: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacy/
  • Translation API: We use MyMemory free translation API, which processes text server-to-server
  • File Processing Libraries: LibreOffice, PyMuPDF, and other open-source libraries process files locally on our servers

9. Your Privacy Rights

9.1 Access & Deletion

You have the right to request:

  • What personal data we hold about you
  • Deletion of your session data and quota information
  • Immediate deletion of uploaded files

9.2 Opt-Out

  • You can clear your cookies to reset session data
  • You can request not to receive promotional emails
  • Browser "Do Not Track" support is not implemented (we still enforce abuse prevention)

9.3 Data Portability

Convertly stores minimal personal data. Your converted files are downloaded directly to your device. Session data is not portable (it's enforced per-session).

10. European Users (GDPR Compliance)

  • Legal Basis: Legitimate interests (fraud prevention, service operation) and contractual necessity (providing the service)
  • Data Retention: Session data is retained for 24 hours; logs are retained for 30 days
  • Your Rights:
    • Right to access: Contact us
    • Right to deletion: Automatic within 24 hours; request removal via email
    • Right to object: You may disable service at any time
    • Right to lodge a complaint: Contact your local data protection authority

11. California Users (CCPA Compliance)

  • Categories of Information Collected: Identifiers (IP, browser fingerprint), commercial information (quota usage), internet activity, and file content
  • Your Rights:
    • Right to Know: What personal information we collect
    • Right to Delete: Remove your session and quota data
    • Right to Opt-Out: Stop using the service

12. Children's Privacy

Convertly is NOT intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will delete it immediately.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be announced via:

  • A notice on this page with an updated "Last Modified" date
  • Email notification (if you have an account email)

Your continued use of Convertly after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, how we use your data, or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us:

Email: privacy@convertly.io (or support email from your account)
Website: https://convertly-web.onrender.com
Response Time: We aim to respond to privacy requests within 30 days

15. Summary of Key Privacy Features

  • ✅ Files auto-delete after 24 hours or on download
  • ✅ No emails collected or sent (unless you provide)
  • ✅ No third-party ad tracking or profiling
  • ✅ No user data sold to anyone
  • ✅ HTTPS encryption in transit
  • ✅ Server-side quota tracking (can't be bypassed by users)
  • ✅ Passwords never logged
